# Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

The program finds two numbers in the array whose sum equals the given target. It checks all possible pairs and returns the indices of the two numbers that satisfy the condition.

## Complexity

* Time: O(n²)
* Space: O(1)

## Notes

Tested the solution locally before submitting it to LeetCode. Tested a typical case and a second case where the matching pair occurs at indices 1 and 2.
